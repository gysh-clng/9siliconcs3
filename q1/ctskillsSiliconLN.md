# Step 1:
The canteen’s ordering process is too slow and inefficient, causing lengthy queues and immense crowding during lunch.

# Step 2:
1. Students do not have a sense of urgency when it comes to ordering.
2. The cashier manually checks and calculates totals, and also gives change.
3. There is no system that assists in tracking which consumables are running out of stock.
4. Such a repeated ordering process results in unsatisfied customers.

# Step 3:
| Sub-Problem | CT Skill | Example Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Students do not have a sense of urgency when it comes to ordering. | Pattern Recognition | Create an accessible menu to all so that customers, especially students, are able to view their food choices before reaching the ordering counter. |
| The cashier manually checks and calculates totals, and also gives change. | Algorithmic Thinking | Make a system that automatically calculates the customer’s total cost and their change as well. |
| There is no system that assists in tracking which consumables are running out of stock. | Abstraction | Establish an inventory system that tracks the quantity of each item and updates it whenever it is sold. |
| Such a repeated ordering process results in unsatisfied customers. | Decomposition | The canteen should implement better procedures like a queue-management system to organize its customers. |

# Step 4
```text

START

Get the prices of the selected food items.

Calculate the total price.

Ask the customer for the amount paid.

IF the amount paid is enough THEN
    Calculate the change.
    Display both the total and the change.
ELSE
    Display "Insufficient payment."
END IF

END
